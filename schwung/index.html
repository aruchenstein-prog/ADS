Questions

Do you use AI?

Yes, and we say so. AI helps us write code and create images faster, which keeps prices and timelines down. The design decisions, the details and the final checks are done by a person, on real phones and screens.

How fast can my site be live?

A landing page takes about a week, brand and website about ten days. Most of the time depends on how quickly we get your feedback and texts.

Who owns the website and the logo?

You do. After the final payment, all files and rights to the design we made for you are yours.

Can I change texts and images myself?

Yes. We set the site up so that texts and images are easy to change and show you how in a short call. Bigger changes we can do for you.

What do hosting and a domain cost?

A domain costs about CHF 15 to 30 a year. Hosting for a site like this is often free or a few francs a month. We help you set both up.
