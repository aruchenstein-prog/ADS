FAQ

Good questions

Do you use AI?

Yes, openly. AI helps us build and create images fast, which keeps prices fair and timelines short. Every direction, every detail and every final decision is made by a person with taste, and checked on real devices.

How fast can my site be live?

A landing page usually takes about a week, brand plus website about ten days. It depends mostly on how quickly we get your feedback and content.

Who owns the website and the logo?

You do. After the final payment, all files and rights to the design we made for you are yours.

Can I update the site myself?

Yes. We set it up so texts and images are easy to change, and show you how in a short handover. Bigger changes we're happy to do for you.

What about hosting and domain?

We help you pick and set them up. Hosting for a site like this often costs between nothing and a few francs a month.
